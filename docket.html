The docket · 1 October 2026

9,265 Palestinians are held by Israel. 4,520 of them without charge or trial.

Each point is one person. The bracket around a group is the process the law allows them: a closed record, an open one, a secret file, or none at all. In Sep 2023 the prisons held 5,088.

  • SentencedTried by a court and serving a sentence. 1,50916% of all held
  • RemandUnder interrogation, charged, or on trial. 3,23635% of all held
  • Administrative detentionNo charge, no trial. A secret file, renewed by order. 3,10133% of all held
  • “Unlawful combatants”Held outside the courts, mostly from Gaza. 1,41915% of all held
