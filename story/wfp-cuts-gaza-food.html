WFP cuts food aid to half a million people in Gaza

Funding for 2026 is less than half of previous years; rations are being halved.

Monday 19 October 2026Gaza Strip1 source

The World Food Programme said it would reduce monthly assistance in Gaza from 1.6 million to 1.1 million people, cut rations from two food parcels per family to one, and cut hot meals from 250,000 to 150,000 a day.

WFP said it needs another $450 million to keep critical operations running in the occupied territory for six months.
